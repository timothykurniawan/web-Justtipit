About us

Built by students who lived this problem first-hand.

We are 5 Indonesian students studying abroad. We kept seeing the same problem: friends back home wanted products that were easy to find overseas, but hard to get in Indonesia without paying too much or guessing if the source was legit.

Why we started Justtip.it

  • Make cross-border shopping feel simple, not stressful.
  • Give clear product options and transparent pricing vibes.
  • Keep it chat-first so ordering is fast and human.
